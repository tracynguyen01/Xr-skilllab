The loop

How it works

  1. Explore

    Walk the scene and notice what is there.

  2. Decide / Inspect

    Choose a response, or check the points the scene asks you to review.

  3. Review

    Read a score or a report built from fixed rules.

  4. Improve

    Practise the same decisions again.

The concept

The bigger idea

  • Immersive environment

    The workplace is a scene you can walk, not a slide to read.

  • Structured assessment

    Fixed rules record what you noticed and what you chose.

  • Guided feedback

    Those rules also name the next thing to practise.

A 3D model is not a digital twin unless it is connected to real data, and these samples are prototypes with no live equipment, sensors, property records, or enterprise systems.
