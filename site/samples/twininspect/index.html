The workflow

Capture. Inspect. Report.

01Capture

In a real deployment, someone on site would scan the home once with a phone or 360° camera. Thermal images and moisture readings attach to where they were taken.

02Inspect

A qualified inspector walks the twin from anywhere, with measurement tools, evidence layers and pre-set flags to review.

03Report

Every confirmed finding is pinned to its exact 3D location with evidence, and the report assembles itself as you work.

Architecture

How It Works

Concept pipeline. This demo uses a pre-built model, not a live scan.

Review flags never write to the report directly. They can only raise a suggestion with evidence attached. Only findings an inspector has confirmed, or found themselves, reach the report, and every dismissal is recorded with a reason.

Limits

What a remote inspection cannot do.

Saying this plainly is how the product earns trust. Every report states the same limits.

  • ✕See inside wall cavities, roof spaces or subfloors unless they were captured
  • ✕Touch, test or operate anything
  • ✕Replace a licensed on-site inspection where one is required
